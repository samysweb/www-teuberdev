abstract: |2-
  Released in December 2025, VNN-LIB 2.0 [7] is the latest version of the standardised language for specifying neural network verification queries. While VNN-LIB 2.0 introduces many new features, including a type system and semantics, one of the most significant changes is the increased expressivity of its syntax compared to that of VNN-LIB 1.0 [2]. For example, queries can now involve networks with multiple inputs and outputs, multiple interacting networks, non-linear constraints and constraints over hidden nodes. It is hoped this new expressivity will help drive the development of solvers that are capable of handling more sophisticated verification problems.
